Chapter 01

Solar
manufacturing,
layer by layer.

Convalt manufactures advanced glass-glass HJT (heterojunction) solar modules. Each module is a precise stack of six engineered layers — assembled for decades of performance in the field.

Scroll to explore the exploded view. Each layer separates to reveal the technical architecture of a Convalt module.

01Front GlassLow-iron tempered glass for maximum light transmission and weatherability.
02Front EncapsulantPOE encapsulant protecting cells from moisture and mechanical stress.
03HJT Cell MatrixHeterojunction cells — high efficiency, low temperature coefficient.
04Rear EncapsulantSecond encapsulant layer ensuring double-sided cell protection.
05Rear GlassTempered rear glass for bifacial gain and structural rigidity.
06Aluminum FrameAnodized aluminum frame for mounting strength and corrosion resistance.

Company

One system,
engineered
end to end.

Convalt Energy is a renewable infrastructure company. We design, manufacture and operate the physical assets of the energy transition — solar modules, power generation, data centers and the recycling systems that close the loop.

Our work spans the full lifecycle: from the cell architecture inside a module to the grid that carries its power, to the data centers that consume it, to the recovery process that returns spent modules to the manufacturing line. Each stage is designed in relation to the others.
